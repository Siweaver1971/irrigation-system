⚠️ The PSU dominates the panel
At 159×97mm it occupies over half the usable area. The relay board, ESP32 and buck converter must fit in the remaining ~90mm to the right and ~55mm below.

// Layout Strategy

DIN rail (top): Terminal blocks on left third, GEYA relay on right third.

PSU (left): Screw mounted directly to panel — 4x M4 screws through mounting feet. NOT on DIN rail.

ESP32 (bottom left): Below PSU on nylon standoffs. Wire routes up-left to relay board.

Buck converter (bottom centre): Between ESP32 and relay area on standoffs.

Right wall glands: OLED, water level sensor, Nextion (future), USB access.

Left wall glands: Mains inlet (PG11), pump cable (PG9), solenoids S1/S2/S3 (PG9).

// Assembly Order

1. Fit DIN rail and cable glands first
2. Mount terminal blocks on DIN rail
3. Clip GEYA relay onto DIN rail
4. Drill 4x M4 holes for PSU feet — test fit before drilling
5. Drill M3 standoff holes for ESP32 and buck
6. Set buck converter to 5V BEFORE fitting ESP32
7. Wire all 12V/signal connections
8. Mains wiring last — qualified person only
9. Power-on test before closing lid

// PSU Mounting Note

Check PSU mounting hole positions
The LRS-150-12 has 4x M3 mounting holes on the base — measure their exact positions from your actual unit before drilling the panel. The datasheet shows them but manufacturing tolerances vary slightly. Mark and drill from the PSU itself.
