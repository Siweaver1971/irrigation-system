⚠ IMPORTANT WIRING NOTES:

1. Flyback diodes (1N5822) — solder one across each solenoid coil, cathode (banded end) toward positive. Essential to prevent voltage spikes destroying the ESP32.
2. GPIO 34, 35, 32 are input-only ADC pins on the ESP32 — correct for moisture sensors. Do NOT use GPIO 36/39 (SVP/SVN) as they share the ADC with internal sensors.
3. All 12V and mains wiring must be in heat-shrink or proper terminal blocks. Do not use Wago connectors for outdoor/damp environments — use waterproof gel-filled connectors or proper terminal blocks inside the IP65 enclosure.
4. Relay common (COM) connects to 12V positive from PSU. Relay NO (Normally Open) connects to solenoid positive. Solenoid negative returns to PSU ground.
5. Moisture sensor cables — extend with shielded 3-core cable to prevent interference. Twist the GND and signal wires together inside the cable run.
